Project Subject:
Student Study Guide & Planner
Project Description:
My website will help students improve their study habits and organize their schoolwork. It will include study techniques, useful resources, and tools for planning study time. 
The website will have three pages and will allow students to explore different study methods and find information that can help them study better.
Reason and Purpose:
I chose this subject because I am a student and I understand how difficult it can be to organize homework and study time. 
The purpose of this website is to provide simple and useful tools that can help students study more effectively and manage their time.
